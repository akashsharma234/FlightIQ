✈️ Airline Revenue & Occupancy Analysis
📌 Business Problem

The airline industry is facing increasing pressure on profitability due to rising fuel prices, higher flight taxes, stricter environmental regulations, and increased labor costs. Despite operating a diverse fleet of aircraft, the company observed that low seat occupancy on certain flights was negatively impacting average profit per seat.

The key challenge was to identify data-driven opportunities to improve aircraft occupancy rates while maintaining customer satisfaction and safety standards.

🎯 Objective

Increase aircraft occupancy rate to improve profit per seat

Analyze pricing strategies across fare classes (Economy, Business, Comfort)

Identify low-performing aircraft and revenue optimization opportunities

Estimate the revenue impact of a 10% increase in occupancy

🛠️ What I Did (My Work)

Connected and queried an airline database (SQLite) using SQL and Python

Analyzed seat capacity, ticket bookings, fare conditions, and revenue trends

Calculated:

Seat capacity per aircraft

Total and average revenue per ticket

Average occupancy rate per aircraft

Performed time-based analysis to study ticket booking and revenue trends

Simulated a 10% increase in occupancy rate to estimate potential revenue growth

Visualized insights using line charts and bar graphs for clear interpretation

📊 Key Insights

Aircraft with lower ticket prices showed higher booking volumes and revenue

Business class fares were consistently higher than Economy across all aircraft

Certain aircraft generated low revenue due to limited fare options or lower demand

Increasing occupancy—even without increasing prices—showed a significant positive impact on annual turnover

💡 Solution & Recommendations

Focus on improving occupancy on low-performing flights instead of increasing prices

Adopt dynamic pricing strategies based on aircraft type, facilities, and demand

Balance pricing to avoid seats being too cheap or too expensive

Improve customer experience to support higher occupancy without compromising service quality

🚀 Outcome

This analysis demonstrates how data-driven decision-making can help airlines:

Improve seat utilization

Optimize pricing strategies

Increase revenue without expanding fleet size

The project highlights practical analytics skills applied to a real-world airline business problem.
